# cstweak on the 3D Pulpit

The Reverend posted about cstweak on his 3D Pulpit page at Voodoo Extreme today, under the headline "Tweak your games!".

<!-- more -->

He writes that my Half-Life Counter-Strike tweaking program is out, and that Tweakmania also has programs for Unreal Tournament and Quake 3, "all with nice intuitive GUIs". I'll take that.

The idea behind all three is the same. These games keep their settings in plain text config files, and most of the settings that matter are not in the menus. So instead of a guide telling you which lines to edit, you get a window with the settings in it:

- cstweak for Counter-Strike
- q3tweak for Quake 3 Arena
- uttweak for Unreal Tournament

All three are written in Visual Basic and are free to download from Tweakmania.
