# Working in Singapore

Notes from my internship at an RFID company in Singapore, spring 2005.

<!-- more -->

## My first RFID application shipped! (6 April 2005)

As I started out as an intern in an RFID company, I started out by playing around with their development kit. As I found this interesting, I started to develop a little RFID tag editor. This program was actually just meant to be for fun, but when my boss saw it, he wanted to include it in the development kit as an example application!

This was of course cool, but I felt that I needed to clean up the code and make some improvements to it before it could be released. After some final testing I think that it is stable enough, although not feature-rich enough. The RFID jungle is huge, and I would like to improve features like the tag manufacturer recognition and tag size/page size attributes. Well, there might perhaps be a later version at a later time...

Now it will be shipped in the package, and this means that it already has been sent to Spain, the U.S. and the Arab Emirates!

## RFID@Asia exhibition (3 May 2005)

Last week I was attending the large RFID exhibition at Suntec City. It was really fun, even though RFID isn't killer fun if you're not in love with a robot, but there are still pretty cool things that can be done. As we are selling a "beginners kit" in addition to our full fledged RFID solutions, some people were like "Hey, we are experts, we don't need that stuff".

As I'm fairly new in the business, it was a bit challenging to answer all the different questions coming in, but no worries!

The other thing is that the applications for our latest customer are due for the final acceptance test this Monday, so lots of work to do...
